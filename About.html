About K-Playlist

K-Playlist is a web-based karaoke playlist platform that allows users to create their own personalized singing experience. With K-Playlist, you can input your favorite song titles along with their karaoke video URLs, organize them into a playlist, and manage them with ease.

The platform lets users explore and manage karaoke sessions more conveniently. You can add songs to your cart, customize your list, and proceed to checkout for a complete session setup. Designed with a sleek interface and dark mode support, K-Playlist offers a smooth and enjoyable user experience for every karaoke enthusiast.

This project was developed by Torigami_2005 as part of a web development portfolio. It highlights interactive design, responsive layout, and modern functionality for music and karaoke lovers.
